About

Since 2022, I've built a global creator strategy with consistent 10x+ ROI, working with partners including The Walt Disney Company, the New England Patriots, Dirk Nowitzki, and American Express. The work is integrated by design: partnerships, content, and performance moving as one system.

I'm also a Visual Effects Supervisor on short films recognized at SXSW and NFFTY. Less a side project, more a reminder of where my standards come from.

Originally from Costa Rica.
